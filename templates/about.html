About This App

The Expense Tracker is your personal finance companion. Designed to help you monitor every penny, this app makes expense management simple, intuitive, and insightful. Whether you're tracking daily purchases or planning your yearly budget, we've got you covered.

Visualize your spending patterns with our advanced charts, filter expenses by month and year, and gain complete control over your finances. Plus, with secure password recovery via email, your account is always safe.
